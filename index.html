Financing details & assumptions

Example based on a $385,000 home with 20% down ($77,000), a $308,000 loan and a 30-year fixed mortgage at a 7.00% note rate. If an eligible buyer receives a two-year 1-1 temporary buydown, payments during the first 24 months are calculated at a 6.00% effective rate. That is approximately $1,847/month in principal and interest rather than $2,049/month, a difference of about $203/month or $4,860 across 24 months. The full 7.00% payment resumes in month 25. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance if applicable, HOA dues, closing costs and other charges are excluded. Actual interest rate, APR, availability, funding, eligibility, buydown duration and terms must be confirmed with Advantage Home Mortgage before use as an advertisement.
